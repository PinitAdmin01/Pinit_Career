# PinIT Consultant Portal: AI Career & Global Education Command Center

The Consultant Portal is evolved from a basic Study Abroad CRM into a comprehensive command center enabling mentors and advisors to support candidates across both study-abroad admissions and global career placements.

---

## Workspace Navigation Tabs

### 1. Dashboard (Overview Command Center)
- **Today's Meetings**: List of scheduled video/consultation sessions today.
- **Students Requiring Attention**: High-priority flags for candidates with falling streaks or documents deficits.
- **Visa Deadlines**: Warnings for student visa locks.
- **Application Deadlines**: Approaching university submission cutoffs.
- **High Placement Candidates**: Top candidates eligible for premium international placements.
- **High Scholarship Candidates**: Candidates qualified for national/merit scholarships.
- **AI Alerts & Summary Board**: Athena AI outputs translating database telemetry into recommendations:
  - *Rahul qualifies for DAAD Scholarship.*
  - *Priya has an 87% chance of admission to TU Munich.*
  - *Three students have incomplete Statement of Purposes (SOPs).*
  - *Visa deadline in 5 days.*
  - *Two students are internship-ready.*

### 2. Student Pipeline
- Stages for tracking candidate enrollment progress: Onboarding, Documents, Application, Visa, Pre-Departure, and Completed.

### 3. Student Intelligence
- Evolved single-screen Candidate Diagnostics Dashboard. Clicking on a student in the cohort registry renders:
  - **Career DNA**: Target career twin profiles.
  - **Career Readiness**: Overall eligibility percentage index.
  - **Communication Index**: Soft skills training reviews.
  - **Resume ATS Score**: Automated scan grade.
  - **Coding Pass Rate**: Coding quest statistics.
  - **Research Index**: Academic publication logs.
  - **Projects Count**: Production hosted applications.
  - **IELTS Score**: Standardized English band rating.
  - **GRE Score**: General GRE scorecard details.
  - **CGPA**: Roster grade averages.
  - **Scholarship Match Score**: Percentage qualification rating.
  - **Admission Probability**: Odds matching target global universities (e.g. TU Munich).

### 4. Career Planning
- **AI Career Goal Path Planner**: Interactive matches shifting advising focus from country selection to goal alignment. Displays step-by-step flowchart nodes:
  - **Goal Selection**: User selected targets (e.g. AI Engineer, Cloud Architect).
  - **Career Targets**: Specific matched corporate profiles.
  - **Required Skills Checklist**: Core technical and math skills needed.
  - **Best Countries**: Matches suitable target countries (e.g. Canada, Germany, USA, Singapore).
  - **Target Universities**: Matches recommended institutions (e.g. TU Munich, Stanford, NUS).
  - **Key Advantages**: Reasons for university match (Strong AI Labs, Low Tuition, Good Visa, High Placement).

### 5. Study Abroad & AI Visa Advisor
- **AI Visa Advisor Console**: Evolved visa compliance tracker providing:
  - **Approval Odds Calculator**: Computes approval probabilities (e.g., 93%).
  - **Missing & Verified Document Audits**: Automatically flags crucial omissions or verifies submitted files.
  - **Auto-Generated Embassy Mock Questions**: Simulates a customized list of interview questions tailored to the candidate's exact target program.
  - **Practice Scheduler**: Connects candidates with live mentors for mock sessions.
- **Country Intelligence Comparer**: Side-by-side comparative grid mapping selected countries (Canada, Germany, USA, Singapore) across Visa ease, Jobs availability stars, PR pathways, AI tech jobs density, and Tuition cost scale.

### 6. Mentorship & Alumni Intelligence
- **Alumni Intelligence Matching Pipeline**: Node flowchart replacing basic roster list. Automatically maps target location, target role, corporate placements, active mentors, and booking tools.

### 7. University Matching
- **Dream, Reach, and Safe AI Classifier**: Evolved admissions categorizer that maps applicant credentials (CGPA, GRE, IELTS, budget, targets) into risk brackets.
- **AI Admission Probability Tracker**: Lists university admission probabilities (e.g. TU Munich 82%, ETH 34%, Alberta 91%) with strategic analysis reasons (CGPA baseline pool, publication indexes, quantitative score matching).

### 8. Scholarship Center
- **AI Scholarship Matching Dashboard**: Auto-identifies student eligibility based on their CGPA parameters. Organizes matches into sub-categories:
  - **Government**: Matches global government funding programs (e.g., DAAD, Erasmus, Chevening, Fulbright).
  - **University**: Identifies university fellowships and merit assistantships (e.g., TU Munich Merit, Stanford Fellowship).
  - **Private**: Flags private foundation grants (e.g., Gates Cambridge, Knight-Hennessy).
  - **Company Sponsored**: Lists company-backed research fellowships (e.g., Microsoft Fellowship, Google PhD Fellowship).

### 9. Documents Vault & AI Auditor
- **AI Document Auditor Dashboard**: Evolved verification panel replacing checklist checkmarks with deep-content AI audits:
  - **SOP Content Audit**: Scores drafts across Grammar (92), Uniqueness (81), Research Alignment (78), Leadership (91), and Overall (87).
  - **Resume ATS Scan**: Grades CVs against target roles (overall score 82/100) and provides actionable checklists to improve impact metrics, skills formatting, and key terminology.
  - **LOR Tone Review**: Audits recommender letters to verify professional tone index and registry signature alignments.
- **AI SOP Builder**: A tailored auto-generation editor console. Inputting a candidate's projects, research experience, target career goal, and achievements triggers Athena AI to generate a complete, high-fidelity draft SOP which can be edited inline by the consultant and locked to the candidate folder.

### 10. Placement Timeline
- **Placement After Graduation lifecycle**: Visually charts the candidate's career pipeline post-admission: Admission Offer $\rightarrow$ Internship $\rightarrow$ Resume ATS Optimization $\rightarrow$ Mock Interviews $\rightarrow$ Placement $\rightarrow$ Career Timeline tracking.
- **Academic Semester Career Roadmap**: Interactive progress checklists tracking Semester 1 to Semester 4 goals (German Language mastery, Research Publication Adapter preparation, Capstone Internships, and SDE full-time placement loops).

### 11. Global Opportunity Radar
- **Radar Match Scanner**: Continuous scans of global hackathons, research assistants positions, exchange slots, and trainee programs matched instantly to student metrics (e.g. Google Summer of Code, DAAD Research Internship, Erasmus Exchange, Microsoft Imagine Cup, and ETH AI Summer School for Rahul's AI target goal).

### 12. Meetings
- Booking scheduler for advisor calls and video sessions.

### 13. Analytics
- High-level charts tracking active candidates, visa approval rates, and admissions success statistics.

### 14. AI Consultant Copilot & Sidebar
- **Conversational Copilot Workspace**: Persistent toggleable sidebar and dedicated portal tab allowing consultants to instantly run complex operations:
  - *🎓 Suggest universities for Rahul*: Triggers match predictions based on CGPA 8.4 & GRE 320.
  - *✍️ Generate SOP Draft*: Informs the consultant that the custom draft is saved to the documents builder vault.
  - *📊 Compare Canada & Germany*: Outputs zero-tuition visa comparison.
  - *⚠️ What documents are missing?*: Lists missing files across active roster students.
  - *📅 Schedule next meeting*: Confirms the 1:1 meeting booking for tomorrow at 2:00 PM.
