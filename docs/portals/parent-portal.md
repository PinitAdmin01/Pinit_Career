# PinIT Parent Portal: AI Career Operating System

The Parent Portal is designed to translate complex technical metrics (ATS, Trust Score) into clear, actionable advice for parents. Instead of abstract numbers, parents receive clear status reports answering:
- *Is my child doing well?*
- *Should I be worried?*
- *What can I do to help?*

---

## Interactive Workspaces Built

### 1. Dashboard (Overview)
- **🚨 AI Emergency Alerts**: Displays visual notification badges at the top of the portal:
  - *Critical Alert*: Attendance below 70% in Computer Networking. Backlog Risk: High. Immediate teacher meeting recommended.
  - *Congratulations!*: Career Readiness reached 90%. Eligible for premium internships. Recommended: Apply to early recruiter list.
- **Employability & Performance Cards**:
  - *Career Readiness*: Integrated 74% eligibility score.
  - *Academic Performance*: Live semester score averages.
  - *Attendance Rate*: 88% overall roster attendance.
  - *Communication Lab*: Soft skills training tracking.
  - *Placement Eligibility*: Checks criteria for placement gates.
  - *Current CGPA*: Live semester GPA.
  - *Mission Streak*: Quests and tasks completion consistency.
  - *AI Health Status*: System diagnostic index.
- **AI Summary Alert**: Prominent translation widget:
  - *"Your child is progressing well. Career Readiness increased by 8% this month. Attendance dropped last week. Communication skills improved. Recommended: Encourage completion of Communication Lab this weekend."*
