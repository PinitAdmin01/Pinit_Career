# PinIT Teacher Portal: AI Classroom Copilot & Insights

The PinIT Teacher Portal goes beyond traditional Learning Management Systems (LMS) by embedding a context-aware **AI Teaching Center**, keeping the sidebar clean while concentrating all pedagogical intelligence in one premium place.

## Operational Foundation (Sidebar Navigation)
The portal provides standard operational tabs expected by instructors:
- **Overview Dashboard**: General stats, roster summaries, and action plans.
- **Class Analytics**: Core grade metrics and reports.
- **Exam Results**: Logs of exam results.
- **Students Roster**: Roster management.
- **Attendance Sheet**: Real-time webcam check-in logs.
- **Lesson Planner**: Calendar scheduling logs.
- **Exam Schedules**: Standard exams calendar.
- **Papers Composer**: MCQ template composers.
- **Answer Sheets & Grade Essays**: Rubric scoring interfaces.
- **Messages & Comms**: Chat channels, notes, and notifications.

---

## 🤖 AI Teaching Center
All analytical intelligence and AI assistance are consolidated under a single top-level workspace containing 10 modules:

### 1. AI Classroom Copilot
- Context-aware chatbot console querying student records (strugglers, placement readiness) or composing syllabus exam papers.

### 2. Student Risk Center
- Predictive backlog risk scores (Critical/Medium/Safe) based on Attendance, Coding, Internals, and Communication, with prescriptive paths and clickable **Parent Summary** meeting alerts.

### 3. Teaching Impact Score
- Instructor effectiveness rating index (91%) calculated from quiz completions and engagement telemetry, with single-click slide balancing optimization hooks.

### 4. AI Classroom Health
- Wellness checks auditing Attendance, Learning, Career targets, Stress telemetry (spikes alert logs), and Assignments.

### 5. Career Readiness
- Placement success console detailing Resume ATS audits (68), Coding tests (91), Communication labs (53), Mock interviews (61), Projects (72), and Portfolio setup (44).

### 6. Skill Gap Map & Digital Twin
- Student-specific cognitive profiles containing learning styles (Visual/Auditory), max attention caps (12 mins), optimal study times, along with a **Skill Gap Map** and a GitHub-style **AI Performance Timeline**.

### 7. AI Lesson Optimizer
- Slides auditor scanning PPT/PDF slide balance to fix theoretical bias by generating quizzes, coding tasks, and roleplay missions.

### 8. AI Assignment Generator
- Compiles multi-dimensional assessments (MCQ, Coding quest, Case study, Roleplay mission, Comm exercise, and Rubric standards).

### 9. AI Intervention Center
- Launch one-click remedial campaigns (Improve Communication, Improve Portfolios, Improve Resumes) that auto-assign exercise bundles to the roster with zero manual overhead.

### 10. Industry Readiness Map
- Tiered placement map tracking candidate alignment with Google Ready (4), Microsoft Ready (2), Infosys Ready (22), and TCS Ready (41) benchmarks.
