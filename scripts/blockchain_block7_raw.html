Invariant: Constant Product Invariant: A trade swapping Δx for Δy must maintain or increase the pool's constant product invariant after fees: (x + Δx) · (y - Δy) ≥ k; liquidity provision mints LP shares proportionally to √k.

Invariant: Tick Boundary Liquidity Conservation Invariant: When current price crosses tick boundary i, active in-range liquidity L must step by ΔL_net; all fee growth inside the tick range must be tracked via global and tick-level fee accumulator checkpoints.

Day 36 Price Oracle Architectures: On-Chain TWAP vs Decentralized Off-Chain Oracles (Chainlink) Stage 3: Advanced Specialization & Scale
Month 2 • Block 8 • 4 Quests
🎯 Spoken Goal & Invariant:
Goal: Solve the fundamental challenge of smart contract computing: The Oracle Problem. Blockchains are isolated deterministic state machines; they cannot physically query HTTP APIs or know real-world asset prices. Contrast the two primary oracle paradigms: 1) On-Chain Time-Weighted Average Price (TWAP): pioneered by Uniswap v2/v3, accumulating price over time (`priceCumulative += price · Δt`) to prevent single-block manipulation; and 2) Decentralized Off-Chain Oracles (Chainlink): networks of independent nodes fetching off-chain price data from institutional APIs, aggregating with median consensus (Off-Chain Reporting - OCR), and writing authenticated updates on-chain. Dissect Chainlink's `AggregatorV3Interface`: `latestRoundData()` parameters (roundId, answer, startedAt, updatedAt, answeredInRound).
Invariant: Oracle Authenticity & Freshness Invariant: An oracle price must be cryptographically authenticated, aggregate multiple independent data sources, and enforce strict freshness bounds: updatedAt ≥ block.timestamp - heartbeat_timeout and answer > 0; stale or unverified prices must revert immediately.
⚠️ Failure Mode & tryIt Bug:
Failure: Consuming Chainlink's latestRoundData() without checking freshness: 'int price = oracle.latestRoundData();' During a network congestion event, the oracle fails to update for 12 hours; the protocol executes liquidations based on a stale price, liquidating solvent users.
tryIt Friction: Implement an audited Chainlink Price Feed Consumer in Solidity 0.8.20: read AggregatorV3Interface; validate that price > 0, roundId ≥ answeredInRound, and block.timestamp - updatedAt ≤ 3600 (1 hour heartbeat); implement fallback to a 30-minute Uniswap v3 TWAP oracle if the primary Chainlink feed is stale; and write Foundry tests verifying that stale feeds revert.
